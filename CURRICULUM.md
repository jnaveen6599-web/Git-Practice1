# Git Mentor Curriculum (project: taskly/)
Stage 1  Basics: status, add, commit, log, diff, .gitignore
Stage 2  Mistakes: amend, restore, unstage, rm, mv
Stage 3  Branching: branch, switch, merge (ff + no-ff), delete
Stage 4  Merge conflicts
Stage 5  Stash
Stage 6  Undo: reset (soft/mixed/hard), revert, reflog rescue
Stage 7  Rebase, interactive rebase, cherry-pick
Stage 8  Remotes: push, fetch, pull, clone, upstream
Stage 9  GitHub: pull requests, review, squash merge, forks
Stage 10 Tags and Releases
Stage 11 Pro tools: blame, bisect, show, grep, aliases
Stage 12 Capstone: full team workflow simulation
